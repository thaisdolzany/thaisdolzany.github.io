About me

Pharmacist and translational oncology researcher

I am a pharmacist with a PhD in Biochemistry from the University of Cologne, Germany, where I studied extracellular vesicles and Lyn kinase signalling in chronic lymphocytic leukaemia. My master's was on peptide-functionalised PLGA nanoparticles. I now work on single-cell and bulk transcriptomics, target prioritisation and drug repurposing in cancer, with public data and code that others can rerun.

I am also Responsible Pharmacist and Medical Affairs & Scientific Lead at a pharmaceutical company in São Paulo, and I teach in the Pharmacy programme at Afya Universidade Unigranrio.

Breast cancer · scRNA-seq · Regulons

BRIDGE-TF

Conserved transcription-factor modules behind malignant cell-state plasticity. Discovery in two scRNA-seq atlases, validation in metastasis and TCGA-BRCA. The protocol was published before the analysis, as a pre-registration.

Melanoma · scRNA-seq · Immunotherapy

Malignant resistance program & T cell exclusion

7,186 cells from 31 tumours (GSE115978). A tumour-level pseudobulk reanalysis cut 5,384 cell-level hits to 117; EMP1 loss after treatment is the robust signal.

Clinical operations · Python · API

Oncology trials with sites in Brazil

1,908 interventional trials and 11,387 Brazilian sites from ClinicalTrials.gov. 72% are industry-sponsored and 73% multinational: a feasibility view by phase, tumour type and state.

ADME · Cheminformatics · Machine learning

Solubility prediction under scaffold shift

9,145 curated molecules (AqSolDB). XGBoost reaches RMSE 0.85 on a random split but 1.23 log units on unseen scaffolds, and error grows as similarity to the training set falls.
