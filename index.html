About me

From the bench to the network.

I am a pharmacist with a PhD in Biochemistry from the University of Cologne, Germany, where I studied extracellular vesicles and Lyn kinase signalling in chronic lymphocytic leukaemia. Over ten years of research took me from nanoparticles to vesicles to networks: today I work on single-cell and bulk transcriptomics, target prioritisation and drug repurposing in cancer, built on public data and reproducible pipelines.

I also work on the industry side, as Responsible Pharmacist and Medical Affairs & Scientific Lead at a pharmaceutical company in São Paulo, and I teach in the Pharmacy programme at Afya Universidade Unigranrio.

What I work on

QC, integration of multi-cohort atlases, clustering and annotation, malignant cell states, regulon inference and differential expression, with Seurat, Scanpy, Harmony, pySCENIC and DESeq2.

Connecting expression programs to druggable targets and existing compounds using perturbation signatures, dependency screens and interaction networks.

Trial registry analyses for feasibility, medical affairs and regulatory work with ANVISA, bridging research questions and development decisions.

Bench background in EV biology, kinase signalling in leukaemia and polymeric nanoparticles, which shapes how I read and question computational results.
